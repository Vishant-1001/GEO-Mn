DATA PROVENANCE

MODEL LIMITATIONS

  • Remote sensing supports prospectivity targeting; it does not by itself confirm underground reserves.
  • Scenario effects are model estimates, not guaranteed causal outcomes.
  • Operational and production records are SYNTHETIC demonstration data; production metrics show pipeline behaviour, not real mine accuracy.
  • Prospectivity is a relative rank within the study area, trained on public occurrence records with unlabelled (not barren) background; no subsurface evidence is available.
  • Forecasts are conditional: trailing 7-day conditions are assumed to persist unless a scenario overrides them.
  • Predictions outside the model applicability envelope require independent validation.
