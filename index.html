MODEL LIMITATIONS

  • Remote sensing supports prospectivity targeting; it does not by itself confirm underground reserves.
  • Scenario effects are model estimates, not guaranteed causal outcomes.
  • Synthetic operational data are explicitly identified where proprietary mine-level data are unavailable.
  • Predictions outside the model applicability envelope require independent validation.
